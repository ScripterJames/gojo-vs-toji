## Spirited Away food-interior inspiration — ONLY ramen/soba and food rooms

**Primary official sources, actually inspected:** [Studio Ghibli's official *Spirited Away* film page and still-gallery listing](https://www.ghibli.jp/works/chihiro/) and [Studio Ghibli's 2020 still-release notice](https://www.ghibli.jp/info/013344/), which lists 50 published stills from the film; [Ghibli's food-and-kitchen museum exhibition discussion](https://www.ghibli.jp/info/011235/). This is **visual-research permission, NOT authorization to redistribute stills as game sprites**. The official film page notes stills may be used within reasonable bounds, but derivative-game inclusion has not been cleared. Additional food/stall still previews surfaced through third-party image search, not the basis for an asset license.

**Practical original-art directions, not runtime changes:**
1. **Ramen counter:** warm amber lantern pools over dark brown structural beams; short timber stools and a deep wood counter with a readable narrow blue/green tiled splash zone; bowls, chopstick cup and a small steam plume grouped along *accessible* counter segments. Keep existing seating, service object IDs and E interaction points.
2. **Soba seating:** low dark wood tables and mats/cushions, compact lacquer dish groups, a kettle and dipping-bowl silhouettes, quieter shadows than ramen. Keep walking clearances around all sides and existing interactions, not an unwalkable decorative clutter collage.
3. **Shared food rooms:** warm food highlights against restrained desaturated background; visually separate preparation, service and dining zones; add repeated bowl/plate clusters without introducing noninteractive props. Maintain tile rhythm and grounding/occlusion layers; any future sprite redraws need original pixels and contrast checks.
4. **Explicit exclusion: 7-Eleven.** It must remain a bright contemporary Japanese convenience store with its existing usable aisles, fridge, checkout, hot-food preparation, smoothie, coffee and E prompt loops. Do **not** apply Spirited Away bathhouse/old-town styling there.
